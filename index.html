Theotown Plugins

Yakka Studios has been creating plugins for TheoTown since 2019, with over 750,000 total downloads and consistently positive feedback from users. We focus on delivering high-quality content that balances usability and aesthetic themes, ensuring a great experience for players as they build their cities in TheoTown.

We are the creators of several popular plugins, such as the Indonesia Transport Pack, Recycling Warehouse, Diagonal Decals, Just Stairs, and more—proof of our dedication to the TheoTown community. We also collaborate with other creators to improve their plugins and offer custom plugin creation services for everyone.

KWGT & KLWP

Yakka Studios is also committed to creating high-quality KWGT widgets and KLWP themes. While we mainly focus on anime-themed widgets, we're also developing original designs inspired by a fictional operating system—OrionOS—featuring a unique brutalist minimalism design language.

Though we're relatively new to this space, we strive to craft visually appealing setups for your home screen—especially for anime fans looking for custom themes on Android devices that support KWGT and KLWP.
