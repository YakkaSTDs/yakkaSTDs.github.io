About Yakka Studio

Yakka Studio is an independent Indonesian studio focused on creating content for TheoTown, including plugins, plugin creation tools, and other resources for players and creators.

In addition to TheoTown-related projects, we also create Kustom presets for platforms such as KWGT and KLWP.

With more than 1.5 million total plugin downloads in TheoTown, Yakka Studio has become one of many trusted creators within the game's community. Our goal is to provide high-quality content that helps players build better cities and helps fellow creators develop their own projects.

This website serves as the central repository for our work, including plugins, development tools, blog posts, resources, downloads, and future projects.
